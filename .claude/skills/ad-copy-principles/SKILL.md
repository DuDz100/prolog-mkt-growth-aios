# Ad Copy Principles — Prolog App

This skill defines the voice, messaging framework, and copy rules for Prolog App paid ads. Load before any copy-related task.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Clear, confident, practical. Not salesy. Not jargon-heavy.
**Personality:** The reliable tool that makes work lighter. Like a smart colleague, not a tech demo.
**Language:** Match campaign language. PT-BR for Brazilian campaigns (natural, not formal), EN for international.

## Core Value Propositions

Prioritize these in headlines and descriptions (customize for your actual product):

1. **Speed** — "Save X hours per week" / "Notas em segundos"
2. **Accuracy** — "Never miss what matters" / "Cada detalhe registrado"
3. **AI-powered** — Lead with outcome, not the technology
4. **Integration** — Works where your team already works
5. **Privacy/Security** — If relevant for B2B deals

## Headline Rules

- **15-25 characters sweet spot** for mobile visibility
- **Lead with benefit, not feature** — "Reuniões mais rápidas" not "IA para reuniões"
- **Include keyword in at least 2 headlines** for relevance score
- **Test call-to-action variants:** "Comece grátis", "Experimente agora", "Ver demo"
- **Avoid:** Superlatives without proof ("O melhor"), vague promises ("Transforme sua vida")

## Description Rules

- **Use all 4 description slots** in RSAs
- **One description per core message:** speed, accuracy, integration, proof/social
- **Include CTA in at least 1 description**
- **Specificity beats generality:** "47 equipes economizam 3h/semana" > "Economize tempo"
- **Max 90 characters** — don't go to the limit just to fill space

## Demand Gen Creative Principles

- **Hook in first 3 seconds** for video — state the problem being solved
- **Show the product in use**, not abstract animations
- **Use real interface screenshots** for static image ads
- **CTA must match landing page promise** — if ad says "teste grátis", LP must deliver that
- **Audience targeting signals:** B2B intent, productivity tools, SaaS buyers

## What to AVOID

❌ "Revolutionary AI technology"
❌ "The #1 solution" (without citation)
❌ Excessive punctuation: "Try it NOW!!!"
❌ Keyword stuffing in ways that read unnaturally
❌ Promising features not yet live in the product

## RSA Performance Benchmarks

Use these as baseline for audit decisions:

| Metric | Below Average | Average | Good |
|--------|-------------|---------|------|
| CTR (Search) | < 3% | 3-6% | > 6% |
| Impression Share | < 40% | 40-70% | > 70% |
| Ad Strength | Poor/Average | Good | Excellent |
| Conv Rate | < 2% | 2-5% | > 5% |

## Copy Review Checklist

When auditing existing copy:
- [ ] At least 3 unique headlines with keyword match
- [ ] Headlines don't repeat the same idea
- [ ] At least 1 headline with explicit CTA
- [ ] Descriptions cover: benefit, proof/social, CTA
- [ ] No Google policy violations (superlatives, trademarked terms)
- [ ] Ad strength is "Good" or "Excellent"
- [ ] Pinning used sparingly (only for legal/compliance requirements)
